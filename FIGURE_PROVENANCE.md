# Figure provenance

The historical Observer Discovery figure assets are **not redistributed in this compact GitHub release**. Their recovered source-file hashes remain locked in `SOURCE_DOCUMENTS.json`, and the frozen numerical tables under `historical/` are the scientific source of truth.

The README uses a text-native Mermaid pipeline diagram only. It is an explanatory redraw, not a historical experimental figure and not evidence produced by the curated reconstruction.

The separately preserved local public bundle retains the historical paper figures for archival review.
