# Claim boundary

## Supported

- A controlled toy-world pipeline can start from unnamed primitive readouts, construct candidate axes, and apply an explicit promotion gate.
- The frozen v0.4 top-axis signature is stable across the reported seed study and is not a single-threshold artifact.
- Label shuffle and volatility-family knockdown eliminate promotion at the default gate.
- Coarser stride and stronger measurement noise weaken distinguishability, establishing interface dependence within the tested design.
- Observer Discovery can supply candidate axes to a downstream Distinction Atlas without requiring author-named observer categories at the start.

## Not supported

This repository does **not** claim:

- discovery of real observers in natural, social, market, biological, or AI systems;
- causal interpretation of PCA axes or readout clusters;
- a universal or unique observer basis;
- global optimality of correlation clustering, PCA, or the promotion threshold;
- empirical external validity;
- that the candidate axes are agents, subjects, or ontological observers;
- proof of Observer Ecology, WDBW, feedback-stabilized worlds, or OGD as a whole;
- byte-identical reproduction of the historical v0.4 runner from the public reconstruction.

The safe phrase is **candidate observer axis under this generator, readout bank, and interface**.
