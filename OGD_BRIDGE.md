# OGD / Distinction Atlas bridge

Observer Discovery is the **front door**, not the whole OGD program.

Its output is a set of promoted candidate observer axes. A downstream Distinction Atlas may then ask which generating distinctions are visible under which promoted axes and interface settings.

```text
Primitive readouts
  -> Observer Discovery
  -> Reliability gate
  -> Distinction Atlas
```

The broader conceptual lifecycle can continue to Observer Ecology and feedback/WDBW, but those stages are **not results of this repository**. v0.5-v0.7 integrated the v0.4 experimental artifact into that larger manuscript story without adding new Observer Discovery experiments.
